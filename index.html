There is room for all of you, My Love.

Ma,

I wish I could sit beside you, take your hand, and let you rest against me for a while.

There is so much around you right now, and you don’t have to turn it all into words before you can tell me about it.

If you feel like crying, there is room for that. If the tears don’t come, you don’t have to force them. If you feel quiet inside and can’t explain why, you can tell me that, too.

I know you’re scared about your dad and about what might be expected of you afterwards.

You can be a nurse and still be a daughter who is frightened for her dad. You’re allowed to ask questions, need explanations, and ask other people to share the responsibility.

And Ma, growing into something difficult can still leave room for your softness.

There is room for the girl who laughs at silly things, teases me, wants a cuddle, and needs someone to look after her sometimes.

You don’t have to bring that playful side forward before you feel ready. I love you in the quiet moments, too.

I’m hoping with all my heart for your dad’s surgery to go smoothly and for his recovery to be steady. We can take the news as it comes, one step at a time.

I want to make time to listen, understand what would help, and support you in the ways I can.

For now, let this be one small thing you don’t have to question:

I love you, Ma.

You matter to me, and I want you to feel cared for through this.
